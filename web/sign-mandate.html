Sign a mandate with your own key

You, the buyer's human, sign the mandate (valtakirja) for your purchasing agent. Your browser makes a P-256 key that cannot be exported and never leaves this device; the gateway only sees your public key, as a did:key. The signed mandate is a W3C Verifiable Credential (vc+jwt, ES256). The gateway checks your signature and countersigns. Back to Sous
