Give your purchasing agent a mandate

A mandate (valtakirja) says what your agent may buy on its own. Valio Aimo's gateway checks every order against it: small orders go straight through, bigger ones wait for you, and anything over your ceiling is refused.

You sign it in this browser with a key that never leaves this device. Nobody else, not even the gateway, can sign in your name.
